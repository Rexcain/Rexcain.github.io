Bio

I am a composer-improviser and game developer. I build tools that let musicians reach unfamiliar instruments, advanced techniques, and new relationships between an instrument and its player. My current research, theREALinst, pairs physically modeled brass and piano with virtual performers who practice on them before they play.

I am an MS student in Computer Science with a concentration in Digital Arts at Dartmouth College (expected 2027), working with Michael Casey and James Mahoney. In 2026 I worked with motion capture as a research assistant in Dartmouth's DREAM Studio. I received a BM in Computer Music from the Peabody Institute of Johns Hopkins University in 2025, where I studied with Sam Pluta, Ted Moore and Bryan Jacobs and received the Richard F. Goldman Conservatory Prize.

I improvise electronic and noise music. In 2026 I toured China with Alexander Wu and Zoe Lyu and made an immersive installation with Chris Garcia at the Hopkins Center for the Arts. I also work as a recording and mixing engineer.
