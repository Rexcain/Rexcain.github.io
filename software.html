Palinmnesia

A corpus-based Markov concatenative synthesizer for improvisers

A real-time human–machine improvisation system. It slices my own recordings into thousands of fragments, maps them into a timbral space, and uses a Markov model to answer my live playing with re-stitched fragments of its sonic memory — less an accompanist than a second improviser.

  • Mojo

Magic Academy (working title)

A pixel-art fantasy game where every NPC is an AI agent

A cozy side-scrolling game set in a western-fantasy magic academy. Players talk to townsfolk by typing; NPCs remember what you say and reply in Townese, an invented language whose voices — like every footstep, breeze and birdsong — are synthesized in real time, with no recorded audio.
